The data

569 patients from the Breast Cancer Wisconsin dataset. Each has 30 measurements of the cell nuclei in a biopsy image, such as radius, texture and concave points.

The supervised model

An RBF-SVM chosen from 7 algorithms by cross-validation. On unseen patients: 98.2% accuracy, 97.6% recall, and 1 of 42 cancers missed.

The unsupervised model

PCA compresses 30 features to 7, then K-Means finds two groups. With no labels at all, the groups match the real diagnosis for 91% of patients.
